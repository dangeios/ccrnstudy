Built from the AACN CSC (eff. 2022) & CMC (eff. 2022) test plans. Rationales cite Bojar, Manual of Perioperative Care in Adult Cardiac Surgery (6th ed.) & Hardin & Kaplow, Cardiac Surgery Essentials for Critical Care Nursing.

☁ Cloud sync

Saves your CSC/CMC progress to the same jsonbin.io account as your CCRN apps so it syncs across devices. Use the same Master Key you use there. First device: paste the key, leave Bin ID blank, tap Connect — you'll get a Bin ID. Other device: paste the same key and that Bin ID.

This app uses its own bin (csc-cmc-study), separate from your CCRN bins, but the same Master Key.
